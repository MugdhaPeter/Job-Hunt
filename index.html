For customer support, live chat, phone and ticketing roles. In Apply by link, choose "Customer support CV" and Claude uses this one.

How this works
  1. Every Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 7:41, Claude searches for new roles that match your profile (you set the search in the scheduled task), skips ones that don't fit, and adds the best matches here with a tailored CV.
  2. You tap Approve on the ones you want.
  3. When you tap Apply to approved now, Claude applies to approved jobs on the employer's own careers site, using these answers and the tailored CV. It never uses LinkedIn bots, never applies twice, and stays under your daily limit.
  4. If a site needs a sign-in, a new account or a CAPTCHA, the job moves to Needs you with a note. Sign in once and approve it again.
  5. Found a job yourself? Paste its link in Apply by link. Claude checks it's open, tailors your CV and applies, with a receipt when it's sent.
Applying needs your computer awake with the Claude desktop app open and the scheduled task set to "Require this computer".
